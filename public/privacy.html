Privacy Policy for SubTrack

Last updated: October 2026

SubTrack is built as an offline-first, privacy-focused application.

Data Collection and Usage

SubTrack does not collect, track, transmit, or store any personal information, analytics, or device identifiers.

Local Storage

All data entered into SubTrack (such as subscription details, costs, and dates) remains strictly on your device using local storage. This data is never sent to any server or third party.

Third-Party Services

SubTrack does not integrate with any third-party analytics SDKs or advertising networks.
