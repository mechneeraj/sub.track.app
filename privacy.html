# Privacy Policy for SubTrack Last updated: October 2026 SubTrack is built as an offline-first, privacy-focused application. ### Data Collection and Usage SubTrack does not collect, track, transmit, or store any personal information, usage analytics, or device identifiers. ### Local Storage All data entered into SubTrack (such as subscription names, billing amounts, and renewal dates) remains strictly on your device using local storage (`localStorage`). This data is never sent to any external server or third party. ### Third-Party Services SubTrack does not integrate with any third-party analytics, tracking SDKs, or advertising networks.
